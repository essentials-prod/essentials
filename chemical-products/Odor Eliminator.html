Product Information

Remove as much organic matter from soiled surface. Spray area where odor is originating, usually the urinals,
toilets, carpets or floors and wall area around these washroom units. Clean area afterwards to remove any stains
left on surface. Product may also be added to mop water to counteract odors effectively and mopped on floor
areas around washroom fixtures. Allow Array® Ready-to-Use Odor Eliminator to remain and dry on the surface.
Pre-test on surface in an inconspicuous area. Do not use if surface discolors.
