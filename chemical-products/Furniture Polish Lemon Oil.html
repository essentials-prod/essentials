Product Information

NOTE: Never use on floors. Protect floors and other surfaces from overspray. The product’s ingredients make a
floor slippery. If oversprayed onto a floor, scrub the floor with detergent and dry thoroughly. Remove cap and hold
container 6 to 8 inches from surface being sprayed. Push button, spray lightly and wipe immediately with a clean
dry cloth. DO NOT allow spray to dry before wiping. Use wherever you want long-lasting beauty. TO POLISH
chair rungs, table legs, or other small areas, spray directly on cloth and wipe the surface. Use in the same manner
to REMOVE STAINS, SMUDGES, and FINGERPRINTS.
