Product Information

Remove gross filth or heavy soil prior to application of the product. Hold container six to eight inches
from surface to be treated. Spray area until it is covered with the solution. Allow product to saturate the surface and remain wet for 3 minutes.
For Hepatitis A Virus, let surface remain wet for 10 minutes. Wipe off with a clean cloth, mop or sponge. A rinse with potable water is required
for surfaces in direct contact with food. This product must not be used to disinfect or clean utensils, glassware and dishes.
