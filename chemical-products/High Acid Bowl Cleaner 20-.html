Product Information

Flush toilet. Add 3 fluid ounces (6 tablespoons) directly to water in toilet bowl (3/4 gallon). Brush to cover all
interior surfaces of the bowl including under the rim. Allow disinfectant solution to remain for at least 10 minutes.
Flush toilet. To clean badly stained bowls, repeat above treatment using 4 to 5 fluid ounces of this product. For use
in urinals, add 3 fluid ounces (6 tablespoons) to 3/4 gallon of water. Apply by brush to all urinal surfaces. Allow
disinfectant solution to remain at least 10 minutes. Flush.
