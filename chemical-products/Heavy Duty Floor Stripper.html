Product Information

DILUTION – Mix with warm water. For light buildup dilute 1:12 - 1:14, moderate 1:8 - 1:10; heavy 1:2 - 1:4.
APPLICATION – Use a wet mop or the solution tank on floor machine to apply solution. Cover an area about
50-60 square feet liberally with solution. Allow solution to work on floor finish for 4-6 minutes before scrubbing.
Keep area wet - do not allow solution to dry on floor. PICK-UP AND RINSING – The used stripping solution
should be picked up using a wet vacuum or a mop and bucket. When using a wet vacuum, it is recommended
that the floor be rinsed immediately to prevent small amounts of residue from drying on the floor. When using a
mop and bucket, the floor should first be soaked with an unwrung mop. This will float the spent solution, along
with the old finish, off the floor. Pick up this solution with a wrung-out mop. Rinse the floor completely with clear
water. Double rinsing is recommended. After rinsing, you should be able to run your finger across the dry floor
without picking up any powdery residue. PROCEDURES FOR REMOVING FINISH FROM ASPHALT OR VINYL/
ASBESTOS TILE FLOORS – 1. Keep floor adequately wet. 2. Operate machine at slow speed. 3. Use blue, green or
red pads for stripping (scrubbing type). 4. Do not dry-strip or wet-strip unwaxed floors.
CAUTION: DO NOT USE ON WOOD, CORK OR MARBLE FLOORS. Blockade areas to be stripped. Floors will
become very slippery when stripper is applied. Excercise caution.
