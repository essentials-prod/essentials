Product Information

Stopped drains: pour about 1⁄2 bottle into drain. Wait 30 minutes. If still clogged, use a plunger.
Repeat if necessary. Plugged basement drains require 3 - 4 quarts and should soak overnight.
Slow drains: pour about 1⁄4 bottle directly into drain. Wait 30 minutes or overnight.
NOTE: Use once a week in open drains for preventative cleaning. Do not splash solution
on hands, skin, or in eyes.
