Product Information

DISINFECTION, DEODORIZING AND CLEANING:
Remove gross filth or heavy soil prior to application of the product. Hold container six to eight inches from
surface to be treated. Spray area until it is covered with the solution. Allow product to penetrate and the surface to
remain wet for 3 minutes (for Bloodborne Pathogens: allow surface to remain wet for HIV-1 1 minute and for HBV
and HCV 5 minutes.) No scrubbing is necessary. Wipe off with a clean cloth, mop or sponge. The product will not
leave grit or soap scum. To disinfect food service establishment food contact surfaces: countertops, appliances,
tables, hold container six to eight inches from the surface to be treated and spray area until it is covered with
solution. Allow product to penetrate and remain wet for 3 minutes (for Bloodborne Pathogens: allow surface to
remain wet for HIV-1 1 minute and for HBV and HCV 5 minutes.) Wipe off with a clean cloth, mop or sponge and
thoroughly rinse all surfaces in the area with potable water. Heavily soiled areas must be pre-cleaned before using
this product. See label for additional directions.
