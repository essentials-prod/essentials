Product Information

General use: Apply to surface and wipe with a soft cloth, spreading and rubbing to be sure
entire surface is covered. Always polish with the grain of the stainless steel.
For heavier soiled areas: 1. Use a recommended general purpose cleaner first, then apply
polish. 2. Allow for a brief soaking period after application until accumulated soil is removed.
3. Turn cloth frequently to keep the clean areas working with the polish.
Notes: Never use on floors and protect floors from overspray. DO NOT use product on food
contact surfaces. Contains petroleum solvent. Wash hands thoroughly after using. Recommend
wearing rubber gloves.
