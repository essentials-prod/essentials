Product Information

1. In a plastic pan, dissolve 1 ounce of product to 5 gallons of water.
2. Place stainless ware and/or silverware in solution for several minutes.
3. Remove, rinse thoroughly, and wash in dish machine in normal fashion.
If silver plated flatware is being detarnshed, line the bus pan or pail with aluminum foil. Dispense solution of
product and warm - 100 to 120 ̊F (38 to 49 ̊C) - water. Place flatware or utensils in presoak solution for 15 - 30
minutes. Remove flatware or utensils from presoak solution. Place in dishmachine racks, rinse thoroughly and
process.
