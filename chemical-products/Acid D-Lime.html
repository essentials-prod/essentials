Product Information

General use: Use this product at 1 - 2 ounces per 2 gallons of water for general lime control.
Heavy lime deposit: Use 2 - 3 ounces per gallon of water. Allow areas of heavy lime deposits to soak for several minutes, then brush surface.
Dish machine:
1. Drain, flush, and clean out machine. DO NOT add lime solvent to dish machine containing warewashing detergent.
2. Close drain valve, fill tank(s) with hot water.
3. Add lime solvent at 4 ounces per gallon of water.
4. Turn on pumps until lime can be brushed or flushed away.
5. Drain tank and flush. Always use rubber gloves if product may contact hands.
Notes: DO NOT let lime solution remain in unattended dish machine. DO NOT use on aluminum.
