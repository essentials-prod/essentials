Product Information

1. Remove excess grease with scraper.
2. Spray or pour 2 to 4 ounces of product evenly over medium-hot oven or grill (300-350oF/149-177oC).
3. Immediately spread product around. Use moderate pressure to scour away burnt-on food soil.
4. For heavily soiled oven and grill you may need additional product.
5. Turn the oven or grill off. Wipe down the surface using a thick, wet cloth.
6. Rinse with potable water.
