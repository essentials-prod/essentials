Sanitizing Non-Food Contact Surface Directions:
Apply RTU Sanitizer as is to sanitize hard, non-porous surfaces using a cloth, mop, sponge, wiper or sprayer.
Rub with a brush, sponge, wiper or cloth. Treated surfaces must remain wet for 60 seconds. Allow to air dry. For
sprayer applications, use a coarse spray device. Spray 6-8 inches from surface. Avoid breathing spray.
