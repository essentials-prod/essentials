Product Information

USE DILUTION: 0.2 – 4 ounces per 1 gallon of water.
1. Dispense or mix proper strength solution of product and warm water in a mop bucket.
2. Post “Wet Floor” signs and, using a clean mop, apply the cleaning solution generously to the floor, overlapping mop strokes. For best results, scrub the floor with a brush, especially if the floor has grout. 3. Repeat if necessary on heavily soiled areas. Pick up soil and excess solution with a tightly wrung out mop, wringing it out frequently, or squeegee to floor drain. Rinse or damp mop floor clean.
4. Dry mop or squeegee floor dry or allow floor to air dry before reuse. Once dry, remove “Wet Floor” signs. NOTE: Floors will be slippery when wet or contaminated with foreign materials. Promptly clean-up spills and foreign materials.
