Product Information

1. Drain and remove loose grease.
2. Fill with water to grease line.
3. Add 1 - 3 ounces of product to 1 gallon of cool water.
4. Bring to low boil and hold for 20 minutes. Brush, drain and flush.
5. Rinse and neutralize with mild acid solution (vinegar and water), then drain.
6. Rinse thoroughly with clean water.
Notes: This product may also be used to degrease concrete, quarry tile, or unfinished wooden floors. Do not use on aluminum.
