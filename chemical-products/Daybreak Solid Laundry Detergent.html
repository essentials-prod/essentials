Product Information

1. Pre-sort laundry prior to wash.
2. Pre-soak and/or pre-spot laundry when necessary.
3. Place proper amount of laundry into laundry machine.
4. For wash cycle, use 3 - 4 ounces of product per 50 pounds of dry fabric.
5. When softening and/or souring, use recommended products(s) at the proper dilution(s). Consult a chemical specialist for proper concentrations.
Note: Recommended to store at a temperature below 100°F.
Canister setup:
1. Lift tab to expose screened cap. DO NOT unscrew or remove screened cap.
2. Place the canister into the concentrate dispenser open end down. Use handle when transporting canister.
3. DO NOT remove concentrate canister until completely empty.
4. To remove empty canister, lift it from the dispenser by the handle, and turn upright to avoid spills. Dispose of canister immediately. DO NOT reuse empty canister.
5. Place new concentrate canister into dispenser.
Note: Dispenser WILL NOT activate unless a canister is in unit properly.
