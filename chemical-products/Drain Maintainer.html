Product Information

Blocked Drains: remove as much standing water as possible. Pour the entire contents of the bottle in clogged
drain. Allow to work overnight if possible. Flush with hot water.
Slow Drains: treat drains on lowest floor first. Every other day move up one floor. Add 4 ounces of product for
each inch of drain pipe. Add at lowest water-use period for best results. Continue daily until system flows freely.
Then add every other week, as a preventative maintenance measure.
