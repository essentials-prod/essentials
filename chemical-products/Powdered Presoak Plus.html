Product Information

USE DILUTION: 1.5 ounce per gallon for light duty, 3.0 ounce per gallon for heavy duty.
1. If silver plated flatware is being detarnished, line the bus pan or pail with aluminum foil.
2. Dispense solution of product and warm 100 ̊F to 120 ̊F water into a bus pan or bucket.
3. Place flatware, utensils, or other ware in presoak solution for 15-30 minutes.
4. Remove items from presoak solution, place in racks for dishmachine, and rinse thoroughly with overhead
sprayer. Do not pour presoaking solution into dishmachine as it will cause foaming.
