Product Information

GENERAL CLEANING/DEODORIZING DIRECTIONS: Apply to soiled area then wipe clean. Repeat for
heavily soiled areas.
DISINFECTION DIRECTIONS: Preclean prior to disinfection. Apply this product until surfaces are thoroughly
wet. Treated surfaces must remain wet for 1 minute for disinfection against bacteria. Wipe dry with a clean
cloth, sponge, mop or tissue (wiper) or allow to air dry.
VIRUCIDAL DIRECTIONS: Follow disinfection directions for application instructions. Treated surfaces must
remain wet for 10 minutes for Herpes Simplex Type 2 and 1 minute for Influenza A /Hong Kong.
NON-ACID TOILET BOWL AND URINAL DISINFECTANT/CLEANER DIRECTIONS: Empty toilet bowl or
urinal and apply this product to exposed surfaces including under the rim with a cloth, mop, sponge or sprayer.
Repeat and allow to stand for 1 minute, then flush. See label for additional directions.
