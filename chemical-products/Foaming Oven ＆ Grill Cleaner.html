Product Information

1. Use on empty oven, either cold or pre-heated to 200°F and turned off. Turn off pilot light and/or electric switch.
2. Holding upright, shake can before using.
3. First, spray holding 8 - 12 inches from the top and bottom of surface, then spray remaining surfaces.
4. Allow to stand for approximately 30 minutes, or leave on overnight with oven door closed.
5. Wipe dry with a damp cloth, sponge, or paper towel. For spots that are difficult to remove, re-treat with a
scouring pad.
6. Rinse with potable water.
7. Once clean, and before using again, re-ignite pilot light and/or turn on electric switch, and heat oven to 400°F
for 10 minutes.
