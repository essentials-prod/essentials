Product Information

Dilution: To remove metal complex finishes and most other polymer finishes, prepare a dilution with hot water
of 10 ounces of product to 1 gallon of water. For heavy build-up and difficult-to-remove sealers, product may be
diluted with hot water (up to 32 ounces of product to 1 gallon of water.)
Application and scrubbing: Apply the hot solution to area using a wet mop or from the solution feed tank of a
floor machine. Apply the solution liberally to an area no larger than 10 feet by 12 feet. Allow 3 - 5 minutes for
the solution to work before scrubbing. In most cases, a green or brown stripping pad is recommended. For hard
surfaces with heavy build-ups, such as terrazzo and concrete, a black pad may be used.
Procedures for pick up and rinsing: The used stripping solution should be picked up using a wet vacuum or a
mop and bucket. Wet vacuum: It is recommended that the floor be rinsed immediately to prevent small amounts
of residue from drying on the floor. Mop and bucket: The floor should first be soaked with an unwrung mop. This
will float the spent solution, along with the old finish, off the floor. Pick up this solution with a wrung-out mop.
Rinse the floor completely with clean water. Double rinsing is recommended. After rinsing, you should be able to
run your finger across the dry floor without picking up any powdery residue.
Procedures for detailing and edging: To remove heavy build-up along base boards, in corners, and around door
stops, etc. Product may be diluted as strong as 1 gallon of product to 1 gallon of water. Protective gloves are
recommended when solution comes in contact with hands.
Note: DO NOT use on wood or cork floors. Some grades of linoleum may be discolored by product. Always
pre-test floor with a small amount of solution before stripping. Blockade areas to be stripped. Floors will become
very slippery when stripper is applied. Exercise caution.
