Product Information

To Disinfect: After surface is cleaned, hold can upright 6 to 8 inches from surface to be treated. Spray until surface
is thoroughly wet. Allow to dry without wiping or allow spray to contact treated surface for a minimum of ten
minutes prior to wiping. When used as a disinfectant on food contact surfaces such as counter tops, tables and
appliances, thoroughly rinse surfaces with potable water. Do not use on utensils, dishes or glasses.
To clean and deodorize: Remove gross filth or heavy soil from surface prior to using this product. Shake well
before use. Point valve opening toward area to be cleaned, press down firmly and spray evenly. Wipe clean with a
dry cloth or sponge.
To control mold and mildew: Apply by holding can upright 6 to 8 inches from surfaces to be treated. Spray
until surfaces is completely wet or let air dirty. Repeat application oat weekly intervals or when mildew growth
reappears.
